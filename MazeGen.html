Glossary:

Cell: The smallest discrete unit of a maze. Here, it is displayed as either a dot (in graph mode) or a box (in walled mode). No walls are ever inside of an individual cell.
Connection: A passageway between adjacent cells. Here, it is displayed as either a line between two dots (in graph mode) or an opening (in walled mode).
Perfect Maze: A maze where there is exactly one route from any cell to any other cell in the maze, no matter which two cells are chosen. In plain speak, this means the maze is fully connected and has no loops.
Update step: A discrete unit of time during which a change is made to the maze.
Neighbor: A cell which is directly adjacent in either the horizontal or vertical direction to the cell under consideration.
Box (in the context of Recursive Division): A set of cells in a rectangular pattern with no walls dividing them from each other.
Random walk: A random walk steps to one of its neighbors at random on each update step.
Uniform spanning tree: A maze created such that, of all possible perfect mazes, each had an equally likely chance of being the result.
